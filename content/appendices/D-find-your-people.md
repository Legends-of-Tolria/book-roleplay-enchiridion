# Appendix D: Find Your People

Places where worldbuilders, writers, game masters and artists share work and help each other:

- [r/worldbuilding](https://www.reddit.com/r/worldbuilding/), a large forum for sharing worlds and asking for feedback.
- The [Legends of Tolria Discord](https://discord.gg/UXd6CVyE3P). If you want to share what you're making and meet other writers, artists and game masters, you're welcome there.
