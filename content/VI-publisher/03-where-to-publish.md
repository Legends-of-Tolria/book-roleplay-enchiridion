# Where to Publish

## Questions to ask yourself

1. Is this free, pay-what-you-want, or for sale?
2. Who's your reader, and where do they already shop?
3. Do you need print, or is a PDF enough?
4. How much of the price do you want to keep?

## Technique: start free or pay-what-you-want

Your first release is about readers, not money. A free or pay-what-you-want release builds a following you can sell to later.

## Technique: one good page

Your store page needs a clear cover, a short description that says what it is and who it's for, a few preview pages, and tags people actually search for.

## Places to publish

| Place | What it's for | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [itch.io](https://itch.io/) | Indie games, zines and PDFs; you set the store's cut | Free to list |
| [DriveThruRPG](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/) | The largest store for tabletop PDFs and print on demand | Free to list; store takes a share |
| [Kickstarter](https://www.kickstarter.com/) | Crowdfunding for a bigger project | Fees on funded projects |
| [BackerKit](https://www.backerkit.com/) | Crowdfunding and pledge management | Fees on funded projects |
