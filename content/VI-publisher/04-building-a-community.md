# Building a Community

## Questions to ask yourself

1. Where do the people who'd love your work already hang out?
2. What can you share regularly without burning out?
3. Do you want a community that talks to each other, or an audience that follows you?

## Technique: share the making

Works in progress, sketches and mistakes get more interest than polished adverts. Let people watch the thing being made.

## Technique: be a member first

Join communities as a fellow creator before you post anything of your own. Help, comment, play other people's games.

## Technique: one home base

Pick one place you own (a newsletter, a Discord, a website) and point everything else at it. Platforms change. Your list doesn't.

## Places

| Place | What it's for | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
