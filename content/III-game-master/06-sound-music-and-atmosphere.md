# Sound, Music and Atmosphere

## Questions to ask yourself

1. Do you want music running the whole time, or only for big moments?
2. Can everyone hear it, in person or online, without it drowning out the talking?
3. Who's going to run it, and will that distract you?

## Technique: three playlists

Calm, tense and fight. Switching between three is easy, and the change itself tells the players something is happening.

## Technique: lights and props

Dim the lights for horror, print the letter the villain left, put a candle on the table. Small touches go a long way.

## Technique: describe with all five senses

The smell of the sewer and the cold of the stone do more than any soundtrack. Pick one sense per scene that isn't sight.
