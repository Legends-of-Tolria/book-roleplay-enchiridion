# Tools of the Trade

## Questions to ask yourself

1. What do you find hardest to make up on the spot: monsters, names, prices, or places?
2. Do you want a tool at the table, or only for prep?
3. Are you running a specific rules system, or your own?

## Technique: roll before the session, not during

Generators are best used the night before. Roll a handful of encounters, shops and names, keep the ones you like on one page, and you have answers ready when the players go somewhere you didn't plan.
