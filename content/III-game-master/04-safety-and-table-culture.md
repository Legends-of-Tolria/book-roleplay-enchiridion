# Safety and Table Culture

## Questions to ask yourself

1. What topics might come up in your game that someone might not want to play through?
2. How will a player tell you something isn't okay, mid-game, without a speech?
3. Does everyone at the table get a turn in the spotlight?
4. What happens when someone's running late, or can't make it?

## Technique: lines and veils

Before the first session, ask everyone what's off the table entirely (**lines**) and what can happen but off screen (**veils**). Write them down and stick to them.

## Technique: a way to pause

Agree on a simple signal anyone can use to skip or change a scene, no questions asked. Tools like the X-Card do this. You'll rarely need it, and having it makes everyone braver.

## Technique: check in after

End each session with two questions: what was the best bit, and what would you like more of? It takes a minute and steers the whole campaign.
