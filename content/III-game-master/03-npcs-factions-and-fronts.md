# NPCs, Factions and Fronts

## Questions to ask yourself

1. Who in your world is doing something right now, whether or not the players notice?
2. What does each faction want, and who stands in its way?
3. What happens in a month if the players do nothing?
4. Which NPC would your players miss if they died?

## Technique: an NPC in three lines

A name, a want, and one thing players will remember: a voice, a habit, a smell. That's enough to play them.

## Technique: factions with clocks

Give each faction a goal and a clock of four to six steps. Each session, tick it forward if no one stopped it. When it fills, the thing happens, and the world changes.

## Technique: the front

A front is a threat with a plan. Write what it wants, the signs that it's coming, and the worst outcome. Show one sign each session and let the players decide when to act.
