# Campaign Notes and Recaps

## Questions to ask yourself

1. Could you tell someone what happened three sessions ago?
2. Which NPCs, promises and loose ends are your players likely to come back to?
3. Do your players read recaps? Would they if they were short?

## Technique: write the recap the same night

Five minutes after the session, while it's fresh: what happened, what they found, what's still open, and the funniest line. That's a recap.

## Technique: track threads

Keep a list of every open thread: the debt, the missing brother, the map nobody read. Tick them off as they close. When prep feels stuck, pick a thread.

## Technique: let players do it

Some groups love writing recaps in character. Give the job to whoever enjoys it, and read it aloud at the start of the next session.
