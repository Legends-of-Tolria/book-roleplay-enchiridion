# Prep That Pays Off

## Questions to ask yourself

1. What did the players do last time, and what would the world do in response?
2. Which scene are you sure will happen? Which ones might?
3. What do you always end up improvising badly: names, maps, fights?
4. How much time do you actually have to prep this week?

## Technique: prep situations, not plots

Players won't follow your script, so don't write one. Prep people who want things, places with secrets, and problems that get worse if ignored. Let the players decide what happens.

## Technique: secrets, not scenes

Write down a handful of things the players could learn, without deciding where they'll learn them. Drop each one wherever it fits.

## Technique: a list of names

Keep ten names on a card. When a player asks the innkeeper's name, you'll have one.
