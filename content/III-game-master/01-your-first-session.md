# Your First Session

## Questions to ask yourself

1. Which game are you running, and have you read the parts you need for tonight?
2. How long is the session, and where does it end?
3. Who's playing, and what are they hoping for: fights, mysteries, jokes, drama?
4. What will you do if they ignore your plan entirely?

## Technique: start in the middle

Skip the tavern. Open with the characters already in trouble: the cart is sinking, the alarm has gone off, the stranger is bleeding on the doorstep. Explain later.

## Technique: a short first game

Plan one location, three scenes and one choice that matters. Two to three hours is plenty. Ending early with everyone wanting more beats running long.

## Technique: talk before you play

Spend ten minutes on what kind of game this is, what's off the table, and how often you'll meet. It saves a lot of awkward sessions later.

## Technique: you'll forget rules, that's fine

Make a fair call, write it down, check it after the game. Nobody remembers the rule you got wrong. They remember whether they had fun.
