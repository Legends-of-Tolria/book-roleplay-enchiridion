# Learning to Draw and Paint

## Questions to ask yourself

1. What do you want to draw: characters, maps, creatures, places?
2. How much time a week can you practise? Ten minutes a day counts.
3. Paper or digital?
4. Do you learn better from video, books or exercises?

## Technique: draw a little every day

Short daily practice beats a long session once a month. Fill a page, then close the book.

## Technique: fundamentals first

Lines, shapes, perspective, light and shadow. They're the boring bit and they're what makes everything else look right.

## Technique: copy to learn

Copy art you love, for practice only. You'll see choices the artist made that you'd never notice by looking.

## Technique: gesture drawing

Draw figures from photos in 30 seconds to two minutes each. Speed forces you to see the pose, not the details.
