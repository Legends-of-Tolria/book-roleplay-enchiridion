# Hiring an Artist

## Questions to ask yourself

1. What exactly do you need: a portrait, a map, a cover, a set of icons?
2. Where will it be used, and will you sell anything it appears on?
3. What's your budget, and your deadline?
4. Whose style do you love, and why?

## Technique: find the style first

Browse portfolios until you find work that already looks like what you want. Hiring for a style the artist already has is cheaper and better than asking them to change.

## Technique: a clear brief

One page: what it is, what it's for, the size and format, a few reference images, the deadline and the budget. Say what's essential and what's up to them.

## Technique: agree the rights in writing

Who can use the art, where, and for how long? Personal use, commercial use and exclusive rights are priced differently. Put it in a short written agreement, even if it's just an email you both reply "yes" to.

## Technique: pay fairly, on time

Expect a deposit up front (often half) and the rest on delivery. Agree how many rounds of changes are included. Credit the artist wherever you use their work.
