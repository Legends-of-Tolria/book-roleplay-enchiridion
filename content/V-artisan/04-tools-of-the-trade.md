# Tools of the Trade

## Questions to ask yourself

1. Do you draw with a mouse, a tablet, or an iPad?
2. Do you need painting, vector shapes, or photo editing?
3. Do you want to pay once, monthly, or nothing?

## Drawing and painting

| Tool | What it's for | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Krita](https://krita.org/) | Digital painting and illustration on Windows, Mac and Linux | Free, open source |
| [GIMP](https://www.gimp.org/) | Photo editing and image work | Free, open source |
| [Photopea](https://www.photopea.com/) | Photo editing in the browser, opens Photoshop files | Free with ads; paid tier |
| [Procreate](https://procreate.com/) | Painting on iPad | One-time purchase |
| [Clip Studio Paint](https://www.clipstudio.net/en/) | Illustration and comics | Paid; free trial |

## Vector and 3D

| Tool | What it's for | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Inkscape](https://inkscape.org/) | Vector art: icons, logos, maps that scale cleanly | Free, open source |
| [Blender](https://www.blender.org/) | 3D modelling, sculpting and rendering | Free, open source |
