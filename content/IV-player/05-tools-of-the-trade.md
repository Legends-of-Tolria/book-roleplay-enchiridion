# Tools of the Trade

## Questions to ask yourself

1. Which game are you playing, and does it have an official app?
2. Do you prefer paper or a screen?
3. Do you want a miniature or a portrait of your character?

## Character sheets and builders

| Tool | What it's for | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [D&D Beyond](https://www.dndbeyond.com/) | Character builder and sheets for Dungeons & Dragons | Free to start; paid content |
| [Pathbuilder 2e](https://pathbuilder2e.com/) | Character builder for Pathfinder Second Edition | Free; paid extras |
| [Demiplane](https://www.demiplane.com/) | Digital sheets and rules for several games | Free to start; paid content |

## Characters and names

| Tool | What it's for | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Fantasy Name Generators](https://www.fantasynamegenerators.com/) | Names for any people or place | Free |
| [Hero Forge](https://www.heroforge.com/) | Design a custom miniature, and download a portrait of it | Free to design; paid to print |
