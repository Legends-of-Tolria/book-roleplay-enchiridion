# Roleplay Techniques

## Questions to ask yourself

1. Do you want to speak as your character, describe what they do, or a mix?
2. What does your character sound like? Do they have a phrase?
3. What do they want in this scene?

## Technique: first or third person, both are fine

"I draw my sword" and "Mara draws her sword" are equally good roleplay. Use whichever you're comfortable with.

## Technique: a want for every scene

Walk into every scene wanting something small: a drink, an answer, an apology. It gives you something to do and say.

## Technique: make others look good

Ask the other characters questions. Set up their big moments. The best players at any table make the scene better for everyone.

## Technique: say yes, and

When someone adds to the scene, build on it instead of blocking it. "Yes, and the guard looks nervous" keeps things moving.
