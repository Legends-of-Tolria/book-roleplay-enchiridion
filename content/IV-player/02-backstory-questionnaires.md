# Backstory Questionnaires

## Questions to ask yourself

Answer five of these in a sentence each. Skip the rest.

1. Where did you grow up, and why did you leave?
2. Who taught you what you know?
3. What's the worst thing you've ever done?
4. Who do you owe? Who owes you?
5. What do you carry that you'd never sell?
6. What scares you more than death?
7. What would you do with a fortune?
8. Who's looking for you?
9. What lie do you tell about yourself?
10. What would make you betray the party?

## Technique: share with your game master

Hand over your answers. A good GM will pick one or two and bring them into the story, which is the best gift a player can get.

## Technique: secrets are fine, surprises are risky

Keep a secret from the other players if you like, but tell the GM. A secret that ruins someone else's night isn't worth it.
