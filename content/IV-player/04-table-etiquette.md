# Table Etiquette

## Questions to ask yourself

1. Are you showing up on time and ready to play?
2. Do you know the rules your character uses?
3. Is everyone getting a turn in the spotlight?
4. Is your phone helping the game or pulling you out of it?

## Technique: know your character sheet

Know what your character can do before your turn comes. It keeps the game moving and the fun high.

## Technique: share the spotlight

If you've had a big moment, pass it on. Ask a quieter player what their character does.

## Technique: respect the table's limits

If the group has agreed on lines and veils, stick to them. If something makes you uncomfortable, say so or use the agreed signal.

## Technique: be honest with the dice

Roll where people can see, or online where the roll is shared. Trust keeps games fun.
