# A Character With a Reason to Adventure

## Questions to ask yourself

1. Why can't your character just stay home?
2. What do they want that they can only get out there?
3. Why would they work with the rest of the party?
4. What will make them say yes when trouble knocks?

## Technique: build for the game you're in

Ask the game master what the campaign is about before you make your character. A brooding loner is a hard sell in a heist crew. A character who wants what the story is about is easy to play.

## Technique: one tie to another character

Agree a link with another player: siblings, old rivals, owed a favour. You'll have someone to talk to from the first scene.

## Technique: leave room

A short backstory with a few open questions gives the game master something to build on. Your character will grow more in play than on paper.
