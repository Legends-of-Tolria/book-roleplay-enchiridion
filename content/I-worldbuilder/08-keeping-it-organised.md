# Keeping It Organised

## Questions to ask yourself

1. Where does your world live right now: one file, a notebook, scattered chats?
2. When two notes disagree, which one wins?
3. Does anyone else need to read or edit it?
4. What do you look up most often mid-session or mid-chapter?

## Technique: one home, one note per thing

Keep everything in one place, one note per person, place, faction or event. Link them to each other. Linked notes are easy to find and easy to keep in sync.

## Technique: mark what's canon

Tag notes as **canon** (it happened), **draft** (maybe) or **secret** (true, but nobody knows). When you change your mind, change the note, not a copy of it.

## Technique: a "recently true" note

Keep a single note of what changed lately: who died, who moved, what burned. Read it before every session or writing day.
