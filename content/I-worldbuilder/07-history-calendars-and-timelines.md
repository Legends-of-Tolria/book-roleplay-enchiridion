# History, Calendars and Timelines

## Questions to ask yourself

1. What happened in the last fifty years that people still argue about?
2. What do people *think* happened long ago? Is it true?
3. How do people count days, and what do they celebrate?
4. What's coming up on the calendar when your story starts?

## Technique: recent history first

Ancient ages are fun to write and rarely matter at the table. The war that ended twenty years ago does: the veterans are still alive, and so are their grudges.

## Technique: history as rumour

Write each historical event as two short versions: what the winners say, and what the losers say. Now your characters can disagree about the past, which is far more interesting than a list of dates.

## Technique: a calendar you'll use

Twelve months and seven-day weeks are fine. Rename a few, add two festivals and one day everyone dreads. Put the festival in the story.
