# Magic, Technology and Their Costs

## Questions to ask yourself

1. What can magic or technology do here that it can't in our world?
2. Who has it, and who doesn't?
3. What does it cost: money, time, health, memory, something stranger?
4. What does it break? Which old jobs, faiths or wars does it change?
5. What can it never do?

## Technique: the cost makes the story

Power that's free solves every problem, and stories need problems. Give each use a price your characters feel, and the hard choices write themselves.

## Technique: decide how much the audience needs to know

Some magic works like a rulebook: readers and players know the limits and can plan clever uses. Some stays mysterious and works best for wonder and dread. Both are fine. Pick one on purpose.

## Technique: follow it into daily life

If healing magic exists, what do hospitals look like? If ships fly, what happened to harbours? One ordinary consequence makes a system feel lived in.
