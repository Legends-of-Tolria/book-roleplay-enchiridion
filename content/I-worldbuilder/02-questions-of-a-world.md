# Questions of a World

## Questions to ask yourself

**Place**

1. What does the land look like from the window of the place your story starts?
2. Where does the food come from, and who goes hungry when it fails?
3. What's dangerous to travel through, and why do people still do it?

**People**

4. Who's in charge, and who wants them gone?
5. What do ordinary people believe about the gods, the dead, or the stars?
6. What do two neighbouring peoples disagree about?

**Trouble**

7. What changed recently, and who's angry about it?
8. What secret would change everything if it got out?
9. What's about to happen if nobody does anything?

## Technique: answer three, not thirty

Pick the three questions that excite you and answer them in a sentence each. The rest you'll answer when the story asks, and those answers will be better for it.

## Technique: answer with a person

Instead of "the kingdom is in decline", write "the tax collector in Harrow hasn't been paid in a year, and he's started keeping what he collects". Worlds feel real through the people in them.
