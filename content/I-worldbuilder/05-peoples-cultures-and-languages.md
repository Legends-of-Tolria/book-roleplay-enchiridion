# Peoples, Cultures and Languages

## Questions to ask yourself

1. What does this people eat, wear and celebrate?
2. What do they think is rude? What do they think is brave?
3. How do they greet a stranger, and how do they bury their dead?
4. What do outsiders get wrong about them?
5. Do they disagree among themselves? (They should.)

## Technique: borrow with care

Real cultures are a rich well, and they belong to real people. Mix sources, change the reasons behind the customs, and avoid turning a living culture into a costume or a villain.

## Technique: a language in ten words

You don't need a full conlang. Pick a sound palette (a few letters it uses a lot, a few it never uses), then make ten words: hello, home, stranger, god, water, war, friend, mother, the people's own name for themselves, and one word that doesn't translate. Names built from those sounds will feel like they belong together.
