# Where to Begin

## Questions to ask yourself

1. What made you want to build this world? A place, a picture, a "what if"?
2. Who is it for: you, a table of players, or readers?
3. Do you need a whole planet, or one valley and the road out of it?
4. What's the one thing that makes it different from every other world?

## Technique: start small and close

Begin where the story or the game begins. One town, the people in it, the trouble they're in. Everything past the horizon can be a rumour until someone goes there.

## Technique: the one-page world

Before any app, write one page: a paragraph of place, a paragraph of people, a paragraph of conflict, and three names you like. If it fits on a page, you can play it or write it tomorrow.

## Technique: build what you'll use

If a detail won't reach a player or a reader in the next few sessions or chapters, it can wait. Worldbuilding is fun, and it's also the easiest way to never start.
