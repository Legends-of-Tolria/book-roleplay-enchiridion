# Maps and Geography

## Questions to ask yourself

1. Who will look at this map: you, your players, or your readers?
2. What scale do you need: a continent, a city, or the room where the fight happens?
3. Does it need to look finished, or only make sense?
4. Will it go on a virtual tabletop?

## Technique: draw the map the story needs

Start with the places your story or campaign actually visits, and the routes between them. Rivers run downhill to the sea, cities sit on water and trade, and borders follow mountains and rivers. Get those three right and a rough map feels real. Fill in the rest only when someone travels there.
