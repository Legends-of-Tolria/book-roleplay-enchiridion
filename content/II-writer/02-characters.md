# Characters

## Questions to ask yourself

1. What does this character want, and what do they actually need?
2. What are they wrong about?
3. What would they never do? What would make them do it?
4. Who do they love, and who do they owe?
5. How do they talk when they're angry?

## Technique: want, need, wound

A **want** they chase, a **need** they don't see, and a **wound** that explains the gap between the two. That's enough to carry a story.

## Technique: give them a choice

Characters show who they are when they choose between two things they care about. Build scenes around those choices.

## Technique: let them be wrong

A character who's right about everything has nowhere to go. A wrong belief gives them an arc.
