# Writing Prompts by Genre

## Questions to ask yourself

1. Which genre do you read most? Start there.
2. Do you want a short piece to finish today, or a seed for something long?
3. What did you love about the last story that stuck with you?

## Prompts

**Fantasy.** A healer discovers every life she saves shortens her own by a day. She's counted.

**Science fiction.** The colony ship's AI wakes you early. It wants to talk before the others are up.

**Horror.** The new house came with a list of rules taped inside the cupboard. Rule seven is crossed out.

**Mystery.** The victim left a note naming the killer. It's in your handwriting.

**Romance.** Two rival cartographers are forced to map the same uncharted coast together.

**Adventure.** The map leads exactly where it says. That's the problem.

**Historical.** A forger in a port city is hired to fake a royal pardon, for someone the crown wants dead.

## Technique: the twist

Take any prompt and change one thing: the hero's age, the place, the century, or who's telling it.
