# Feedback and Critique

## Questions to ask yourself

1. What kind of feedback do you want right now: encouragement, big picture, or line by line?
2. Is this draft ready for strangers, or only for friends?
3. Can you hear "this didn't work for me" without arguing?

## Technique: ask a specific question

"Is it good?" gets you nothing. "Where did you get bored?" and "What did you think would happen next?" get you gold.

## Technique: look for patterns

One reader's opinion is a data point. Three readers stopping on the same page is a problem worth fixing.

## Technique: give to get

Critique circles run on trade. Give thoughtful feedback and you'll get it back, and you'll learn as much from reading others' drafts as from your own.
