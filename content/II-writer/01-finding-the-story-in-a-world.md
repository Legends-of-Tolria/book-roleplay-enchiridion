# Finding the Story in a World

## Questions to ask yourself

1. Who in this world wants something badly and can't have it?
2. Where is the world under the most pressure right now?
3. Whose life changes most if the big event happens?
4. What part of the world would you most like to see through someone's eyes?

## Technique: a world is a setting, not a story

A story starts when one person wants something and the world gets in the way. Look for the place where your world's biggest problem lands on one ordinary life.

## Technique: show the world through what it costs

Readers don't need the history lecture. They need to see what the history did to your character: the scar, the debt, the empty chair at dinner.

## Technique: leave most of it out

The details you invented and never used still shaped the story. Trust that they did, and let them stay in your notes.
