# Tools of the Trade

## Questions to ask yourself

1. Do you write in long sessions at a desk, or in bits on your phone?
2. Do you need your notes and your draft side by side?
3. Is this for a book you'll publish, or for your own pleasure?
4. Do you want your files on your device, or in the cloud?

## Technique: the tool matters less than the habit

Any of these will hold a novel. Pick one, write in it for a month, and switch only if something keeps getting in the way.

## Writing apps

| --- | --- | --- |
| [LibreOffice Writer](https://www.libreoffice.org/) | A full word processor for any computer | Free, open source |
