# Plot and Structure

## Questions to ask yourself

1. What does your main character want at the start, and do they get it?
2. What's the worst thing that could happen to them? Does it?
3. Where does the story end, and is that ending earned?
4. Which scene are you most excited to write?

## Technique: three acts are plenty

Setup, confrontation, resolution. Something knocks the character out of their normal life, things get harder until all seems lost, and then they make a choice that ends it. Most structures are this one with more labels.

## Technique: every scene changes something

At the end of each scene, something should be different: a secret out, a door shut, a friend lost. If nothing changed, cut it or merge it.

## Technique: plan or discover, both work

Some writers outline every beat, some find the story as they go. Try a light outline (one line per chapter) and change it freely.
